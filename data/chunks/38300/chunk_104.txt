For each HARQ-ACK codebook, more than one PUCCH for HARQ-ACK transmission within a slot is supported. Each PUCCH is limited within one sub-slot, and the sub-slot pattern is configured per HARQ-ACK codebook. 5.3.5.5 Prioritization of overlapping transmissions PUSCH and PUCCH can be associated with a priority (high/low) by RRC or L1 signalling.
