A specific mapping is configured: - for each F1-U GTP-U tunnel; - for non-UE associated F1AP messages; - for UE-associated F1AP messages; - for non-F1 traffic. Multiple mappings can contain the same BH RLC channel and/or next-hop BAP address and/or BAP routing ID. In case the IAB-MT is NR-dual-connected (SA mode only), the mapping may include two separate BH RLC channels, where the two BH RLC channels are established toward different parent nodes. In case the IAB-node is configured with multiple IP addresses for F1-C on the NR leg, multiple mappings can be configured for non-UE-associated F1AP messages or UE-associated F1AP messages. The appropriate mapping is selected based on the IAB-node's implementation. These traffic mapping configurations are performed via F1AP.
