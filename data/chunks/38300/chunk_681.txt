Upon UE's transition from RRC_IDLE to RRC_CONNECTED, the gNB serving the UE should ensure that it does not release an already configured signalling-based QoE measurement configuration for the sake of configuring a new management-based QoE measurement configuration. QoE measurements for ongoing sessions should be continued when switching between 5GC Shared MBS traffic delivery and 5GC Individual MBS traffic delivery modes for MBS multicast. When the UE resumes the connection with a gNB that does not support QMC, the UE releases all configured application layer measurement configurations.
