When the default SLRB is used for the QoS flow and the SL-CAPC of the default SLRB is not configured: - if the QoS flow is associated with standardized PQI, the UE derives SL-CAPC for the flow directly from the table below - if the QoS flow is associated with non-standardized PQI, the UE may select the SL-CAPC of the standardized PQI having the closest PDB. The UE then selects the lowest SL-CAPC priority level (highest SL-CAPC value) among the QoS flows to determine the SL-CAPC for the default SLRB. Table 16.9.9-1: Mapping between Channel Access Priority Classes for SL-U and PQI When performing Type 1 LBT for the transmission of a sidelink TB, the SL UE shall select the SL-CAPC as follows: - If only SL MAC CE(s) are included in the SL TB, the highest priority SL-CAPC is used; or - If SCCH SDU(s) are included in the SL TB, the highest priority SL-CAPC is used; or - The lowest priority SL-CAPC of the SL logical channel(s) with MAC SDU(s) multiplexed in the TB is used
