How the measurements are actually executed in the physical layer by an implementation (inputs A and Layer 1 filtering) is not constrained by the standard. - A1: measurements (i.e. beam specific measurements) reported by layer 1 to layer 3 after layer 1 filtering. - Beam Consolidation/Selection: beam specific measurements are consolidated to derive cell quality. The behaviour of the Beam consolidation/selection is standardised and the configuration of this module is provided by RRC signalling. Reporting period at B equals one measurement period at A1. - B: a measurement (i.e. cell quality) derived from beam-specific measurements reported to layer 3 after beam consolidation/selection. - Layer 3 filtering for cell quality: filtering performed on the measurements provided at point B.
