At initial setup, the KgNB is derived directly from KAMF, and is then considered to be associated with a virtual NH parameter with NCC value equal to zero. At initial setup, the derived NH value is associated with the NCC value one. On handovers, the basis for the KgNB that will be used between the UE and the target gNB, called KgNB*, is derived from either the currently active KgNB or from the NH parameter. If KgNB* is derived from the currently active KgNB, this is referred to as a horizontal key derivation and is indicated to UE with an NCC that does not increase. If the KgNB* is derived from the NH parameter, the derivation is referred to as a vertical key derivation and is indicated to UE with an NCC increase.
