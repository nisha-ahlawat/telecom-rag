For UE location verification based on multi-RTT with single satellite in NTN, at least the following UE and gNB measurements specified in TS 38.215 [59] are reported: gNB receive-transmit time difference at the uplink time synchronization reference point, UE receive-transmit time difference, UE receive-transmit time difference subframe offset and DL timing drift.
