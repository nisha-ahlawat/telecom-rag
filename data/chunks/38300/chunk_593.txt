Measurement rules for cell re-selection based on timing information and location information are specified in clause 5.2.4.2 in TS 38.304 [10]. 16.14.4 Switchover 16.14.4.1 Definitions A feeder link switchover is the procedure where the feeder link is changed from a source NTN Gateway to a target NTN Gateway for a specific NTN payload. The feeder link switchover is a Transport Network Layer procedure. Service link switch refers to a change of the serving NTN payload. Both hard and soft feeder link switchover are supported in NTN. 16.14.4.2 Assumptions A feeder link switch over may result in transferring the established connection for the affected UEs between two gNBs. For soft feeder link switch over, an NTN payload is able to connect to more than one NTN Gateway during a given period, i.e. a temporary overlap can be ensured during the transition between the feeder links.
