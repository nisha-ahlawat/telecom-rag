QoE measurement collection is supported for the following service types in NR cells: - QoE Measurement Collection for DASH streaming services; - QoE Measurement Collection for MTSI services; - QoE Measurement Collection for VR services. The QoE Measurement Collection function also supports collection of QoE measurements for any of the supported service types carried by the MBS communication service.
