If the side control configuration is removed, the NCR-Fwd ceases its amplifying-and-forwarding function. MAC CE indications can be used to configure the backhaul link and the access link of the NCR-Fwd as specified in TS 38.321 [6]. When the NCR-MT is in RRC_CONNECTED state, the NCR-Fwd may amplify-and-forward RF signals based on the side control information received from the gNB.
