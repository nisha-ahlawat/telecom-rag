The UE may skip monitoring of SCI for data reception during SL DRX inactive time. The actual parameters supported for each cast type (unicast, groupcast, broadcast) are specified in the following clauses. The SL active time of the RX UE includes the time in which any of its applicable SL on-duration timer(s), SL inactivity-timer(s) or SL HARQ retransmission timer(s) (for any of unicast, groupcast, or broadcast) are running. In addition, the slots associated with announced periodic transmissions by the TX UE and the time in which a UE is expecting CSI report following a CSI request (for unicast) are considered as SL active time of the RX UE.
