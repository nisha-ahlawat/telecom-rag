The physical layer transport services are described by how and with what characteristics data are transferred over the radio interface. An adequate term for this is "Transport Channel". This should be clearly separated from the classification of what is transported, which relates to the concept of logical channels at MAC sublayer. Downlink transport channel types are: 1.
