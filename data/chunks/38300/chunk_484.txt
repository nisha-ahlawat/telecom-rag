For a SL DRB, sidelink packet duplication is either (pre)configured in the bearer configuration if the TX profile indicates backward compatibility is not needed, or is decided by the TX UE if the TX profile indicates backward compatibility is needed. For applicable SL SRBs, whether to use duplication is decided by the TX UE. In unicast, the TX UE sends the duplication configuration to the RX UE in PC5-RRC.
