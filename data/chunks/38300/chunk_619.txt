For an RRC_CONNECTED UE, event-triggered reporting of the Timing Advance is supported. 16.19.3 Mobility 16.19.3.1 Mobility in RRC_IDLE and RRC_INACTIVE The same principles as described in 9.2.1 apply to mobility in RRC_IDLE for ATG and the same principles as described in 9.2.2 apply to mobility in RRC_INACTIVE for ATG unless hereunder specified. The gNB reference location of the serving cell and neighbour cell(s) provided in SIB22 can be used for the UE to perform the idle/inactive measurement and mobility. The UE can determine whether a network is for ATG connectivity implicitly by the existence of cellBarredATG in SIB1. 16.19.3.2 Mobility in RRC_CONNECTED 16.19.3.2.1 Handover The same principle as described in 9.2.3.2 applies to ATG unless hereunder specified. 16.19.3.2.2 Conditional Handover The same principle as described in 9.2.3.4 applies to ATG unless hereunder specified.
