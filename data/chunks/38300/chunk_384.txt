The event may occur more than once. The solution for the problem may consist of the following steps: 1) Statistics regarding inter-system ping-pong occurrences are collected by the responsible node; 2) Coverage verification is performed to check if the mobility to other system was inevitable. The statistics regarding ping-pong occurrence may be based on evaluation of the UE History Information IE in the HANDOVER REQUIRED message.
