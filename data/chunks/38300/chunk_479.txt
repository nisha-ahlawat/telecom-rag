 In addition, a COT initiating UE may transmit an additional pair of source/destination ID in either unicast, groupcast, or broadcast. In this case, the responding UE can utilize a COT shared by a COT initiating UE if the destination/source (for unicast) or destination (for groupcast/broadcast) of the responding UE transmission match what is carried in the additional ID(s). In all cases, a UE's transmissions in a shared COT should have SL-CAPC value that is equal to or smaller than the SL-CAPC value indicated in the COT sharing information. In order to satisfy the COT sharing requirements, a responding UE may perform an enhanced LCP procedure as specified in 38.321 [6]. When receiving multiple COT sharing indications from different COT initiators, it is up to UE implementation which shared COT is used. 16.9.9.5 Resource Allocation A UE using mode 2 resource allocation may support resource selection enhancements to avoid LBT blocking and increase COT resource utilization.
