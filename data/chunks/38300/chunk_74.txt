The only approach visible to AS, and hence the only approach that is addressed in AS specifications is the approach of using CAGs. 4.9 Network-Controlled Repeaters 4.9.1 Architecture A Network-Controlled Repeater node, referred to as NCR-node, is an RF repeater that enables wireless amplifying-and-forwarding functionality in NG-RAN. The NCR-node is capable of receiving and applying side control information from a gNB with additional functionality to support Network-Controlled Repeater.
