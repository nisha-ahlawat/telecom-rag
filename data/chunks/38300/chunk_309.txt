NG-RAN ensures that SCells mapped to PUCCH SCell are deactivated before the PUCCH SCell is changed or removed. When reconfiguring the set of serving cells: - SCells added to the set are initially activated or deactivated; - SCells which remain in the set (either unchanged or reconfigured) do not change their activation status (activated or deactivated). At handover, LTM cell switch execution or connection resume from RRC_INACTIVE: - SCells are activated or deactivated. To enable reasonable UE battery consumption when BA is configured, only one UL BWP for each uplink carrier and one DL BWP or only one DL/UL BWP pair can be active at a time in an active serving cell, all other BWPs that the UE is configured with being deactivated.
