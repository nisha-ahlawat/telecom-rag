If the split SRB1 with duplication is not configured at the L2 MP Remote UE, the L2 MP Remote UE using SL indirect path sends PC5-RRC message to the L2 MP Relay UE. Upon receiving the PC5-RRC message, the L2 MP Relay UE in RRC_IDLE or RRC_INACTIVE initiates a Uu RRC connection establishment or an RRC connection resume. If the split SRB1 with duplication is configured, the L2 MP Remote UE sends the RRCReconfigurationComplete message via the L2 MP Relay UE. The RRCReconfigurationComplete message triggers that the L2 U2N Relay UE in RRC_IDLE or RRC_INACTIVE initiates a Uu RRC connection establishment or an RRC connection resume for sending the RRCReoncfigurationComplete message to the gNB.
