This assistance information can be used by the gNB to configure DRX to enable better UE power saving. In addition, 5GC may provide the following information through NG-U as specified in clause 5.37.5.2 of TS 23.501[3]: - Indication of End of Data Burst in the GTP-U header of the last PDU in downlink. This information can be used by the gNB to push the UE back to sleep when possible. In the uplink, the UE needs to be able to identify PDU Sets and Data Bursts dynamically, including PSI.
