As specified in TS 23.247 [45], the gNB may receive from the 5GC MBS Assistance Information associated with a multicast MBS session for a UE, which assists the gNB in configuring the UE properly. The MBS Assistance Information indicates that the UE is expected to require dedicated resources very frequently. Based on this information, the gNB may decide the RRC state of the UE. The QoS requirements of the multicast session apply regardless of the RRC state within which the UE receives multicast session data. 16.10.5.2 Configuration A UE can be configured to receive data of MBS multicast session only in RRC_CONNECTED state or RRC_INACTIVE state.
