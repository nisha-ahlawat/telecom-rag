For NR sidelink communication, the UE performs sidelink transmissions only on a single carrier. 16.9.3.2 Scheduled Resource Allocation NG-RAN can dynamically allocate resources to the UE via the SL-RNTI on PDCCH(s) for NR sidelink communication. In addition, NG-RAN can allocate sidelink resources to a UE with two types of configured sidelink grants: - With type 1, RRC directly provides the configured sidelink grant only for NR sidelink communication; - With type 2, RRC defines the periodicity of the configured sidelink grant while PDCCH can either signal and activate the configured sidelink grant, or deactivate it.
