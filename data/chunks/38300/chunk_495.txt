If the UE receiving data of MBS multicast session in RRC_INACTIVE state in a cell is notified to stop monitoring PDCCH addressed by G-RNTI for all the joined multicast sessions, the UE does not monitor PDCCH addressed by Multicast MCCH-RNTI until the group notification is received. Upon reception of the group notification that does not indicate multicast reception in RRC_INACTIVE state, the UEs reconnect to the network or resume the connection and transition to RRC_CONNECTED state from either RRC_IDLE state or RRC_INACTIVE state.
