For intra- and inter-AMF NG-based handover, the AMF provides the Aerial UE subscription information to the target NG-RAN node after the handover procedure. 16.18.3 Altitude-based Reporting for Aerial UE Communication An Aerial UE can be configured with altitude-dependent, event-based measurement reporting (i.e., eventH1 and eventH2 as defined in TS 38.331 [12]). An Aerial UE sends a measurement report when its altitude becomes higher or lower than configured threshold. The UE includes its altitude and location information in the measurement report if configured to do so by NG-RAN.
