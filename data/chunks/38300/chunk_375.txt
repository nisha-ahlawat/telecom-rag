The "UE reported timer" above indicates the time elapsed since the last handover initialisation until connection failure or the time elapsed since the CHO execution until connection failure. In case of Too Early Handover or Handover to Wrong Cell, the NG-RAN node receiving the failure indication may inform the NG-RAN node controlling the cell where the mobility configuration caused the failure by means of the Handover Report procedure over Xn or the Uplink RAN Configuration Transfer procedure over NG. This may include the RLF report. For MRO analysis, in case of RLFs or handover failures, the source gNB may take into account the information regarding the LBT failures occurred in UL and DL for a specific UE. For UL, the information may be collected and provided by the UE to the network in the RLF report. For DL, the information may be collected at the source and target gNBs.
