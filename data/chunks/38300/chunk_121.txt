In Uplink, the following connections between logical channels and transport channels exist: - CCCH can be mapped to UL-SCH; - DCCH can be mapped to UL- SCH; - DTCH can be mapped to UL-SCH. 6.2.4 HARQ The HARQ functionality ensures delivery between peer entities at Layer 1. A single HARQ process supports one TB when the physical layer is not configured for downlink/uplink spatial multiplexing, and when the physical layer is configured for downlink/uplink spatial multiplexing, a single HARQ process supports one or multiple TBs. 6.3 RLC Sublayer 6.3.1 Transmission Modes The RLC sublayer supports three transmission modes: - Transparent Mode (TM); - Unacknowledged Mode (UM); - Acknowledged Mode (AM).
