If Paging Cause is included in the Paging message, a UE in RRC_IDLE or RRC_INACTIVE state may use the Paging Cause as per TS 23.501[3]. To reduce the probability of potential paging loss due to incomplete UE Radio Capability for Paging, the gNB may update the UE Radio Capability for Paging towards the AMF during the initial context setup procedure as specified in TS 38.413 [26]. Paging optimization for UEs in CM_IDLE: at UE context release, the NG-RAN node may provide the AMF with a list of recommended cells and NG-RAN nodes as assistance info for subsequent paging. The AMF may also provide Paging Attempt Information consisting of a Paging Attempt Count and the Intended Number of Paging Attempts and may include the Next Paging Area Scope.
