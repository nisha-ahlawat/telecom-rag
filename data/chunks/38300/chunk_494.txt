A notification mechanism is used to announce the change of the multicast MCCH contents due to multicast session modification or session deactivation or due to neighbouring cell information modification. The scheduling information for multicast MCCH reception is provided via SIB24 and optionally via RRCRelease message. When there is temporarily no data to be sent to the UEs for a multicast session that is active, the gNB may move the UE to RRC_INACTIVE state. When an MBS multicast session is deactivated, the gNB may move the UE in RRC_CONNECTED state to RRC_IDLE or RRC_INACTIVE state.
