To avoid blocking, a UE may avoid selection of a number of consecutive resources (up to UE implementation) before a reserved resource. Furthermore, a UE may avoid selection of a number of consecutive resources (up to UE implementation) after a reserved resource. A UE using mode 2 resource allocation operating in a resource pool of resources without PSFCH configured can support resource selection for multiple consecutive slot transmission (MCSt). Mode 2 resource allocation operating in a resource pool of resources with PSFCH configured can be used for Multi-TB MCSt with single slot candidate resources.
