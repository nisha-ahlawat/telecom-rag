Sidelink Radio bearers (SLRB) are categorized into two groups: sidelink data radio bearers (SL DRB) for user plane data and sidelink signalling radio bearers (SL SRB) for control plane data. Separate SL SRBs using different SCCHs are configured for PC5-RRC and PC5-S signalling respectively. 16.9.2.2 MAC The MAC sublayer provides the following services and functions over the PC5 interface in addition to the services and functions specified in clause 6.2.1: - Radio resource selection; - Packet filtering; - Priority handling between uplink and sidelink transmissions for a given UE; - Sidelink CSI reporting.
