Late Data Forwarding: data forwarding that is initiated after the source NG-RAN node knows that the UE has successfully accessed a target NG-RAN node. MBS Radio Bearer: A radio bearer configured for MBS delivery. Mobile-IAB cell: a cell of a mobile IAB-DU.
