For DRBs, either UM or AM mode are used. 6.3.2 Services and Functions The main services and functions of the RLC sublayer depend on the transmission mode and include: - Transfer of upper layer PDUs; - Sequence numbering independent of the one in PDCP (UM and AM); - Error Correction through ARQ (AM only); - Segmentation (AM and UM) and re-segmentation (AM only) of RLC SDUs; - Reassembly of SDU (AM and UM); - Duplicate Detection (AM only); - RLC SDU discard (AM and UM); - RLC re-establishment; - Protocol error detection (AM only). 6.3.3 ARQ The ARQ within the RLC sublayer has the following characteristics: - ARQ retransmits RLC SDUs or RLC SDU segments based on RLC status reports; - Polling for RLC status report is used when needed by RLC; -
