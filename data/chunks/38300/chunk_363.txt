Each sub-configuration corresponds to a spatial domain adaptation pattern (subsets of available spatial elements) and/or a power offset between PDSCH and CSI-RS. 15.4.3 O&M requirements Operators should be able to configure the energy saving function. The configured information should include: - The ability of an NG-RAN node to perform autonomous cell switch-off; - The ability of an NG-RAN node to request the re-activation of a configured list of inactive cells owned by a peer NG-RAN node.
