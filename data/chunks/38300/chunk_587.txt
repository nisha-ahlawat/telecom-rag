A TAC change in the System Information is under network control, i.e. it may not be exactly synchronised with real-time illumination of beams on ground. For the NTN-TN mobility, the network may broadcast cell information on NR TN and EUTRA TN coverage areas in SIB25. This is supported for Earth-Fixed, Quasi-Earth-fixed and Earth-Moving cells. The coverage information consists in a list of geographical TN areas, with associated frequency information also indicated. UE can skip TN measurement based on the broadcast TN coverage information. The UE can determine the network type (terrestrial or non-terrestrial) implicitly by the existence of cellBarredNTN in SIB1. The NTN ephemeris is provided in SIB19.
