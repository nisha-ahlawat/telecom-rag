Enhancements of public warning system (ePWS) enable broadcast of language-independent content and notifications to UEs with no user interface or with a user interface that is incapable of displaying text, see clause 9 in TS 22.268 [15] and TS 23.041 [54]. ETWS/CMAS notifications with ePWS functionality use the same AS mechanisms as ETWS/CMAS. KPAS and EU-Alert are public warning systems developed for the delivery of multiple, concurrent warning notifications (see TS 22.268 [15]). KPAS and EU-Alert uses the same AS mechanisms as CMAS. Therefore, the NR procedures defined for CMAS equally apply for KPAS and EU-Alert. 16.5 Emergency Services 16.5.1 Overview NG-RAN provides support for Emergency Services either directly or through fallback mechanisms towards E-UTRA.
