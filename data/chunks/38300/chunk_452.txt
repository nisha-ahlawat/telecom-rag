Reflective QoS is not supported over the PC5 interface. 16.9.2.6 RRC The RRC sublayer provides the following services and functions over the PC5 interface: - Transfer of a PC5-RRC message between peer UEs; - Maintenance and release of a PC5-RRC connection between two UEs; - Detection of sidelink radio link failure for a PC5-RRC connection; - Measurement configuration and reporting related to sidelink. A PC5-RRC connection is a logical connection between two UEs for a pair of Source and Destination Layer-2 IDs which is considered to be established after a corresponding PC5 unicast link is established as specified in TS 23.287 [40].
