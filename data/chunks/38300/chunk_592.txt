For a UE in connected mode, it is up to UE implementation whether to perform NTN neighbour cell measurements on a cell included in the measurement configuration, but without corresponding satellite information in measurement configuration or in SIB19. UE can perform time-based and location-based measurements on neighbour cells in RRC_IDLE/RRC_INACTIVE: - The timing and location information associated to the serving cell is provided in SIB19; - Timing information refers to the UTC time when the serving cell stops serving the current geographical area; - Location information refers: - In the (quasi-)Earth fixed cell scenario, to the reference location of the serving cell and a distance threshold to the reference location. - In the Earth moving cell scenario, to the reference location of the serving cell at the epoch time and a distance threshold to the reference location.
