Similarly, a gNB with knowledge of a KgNB, shared with a UE, is unable to predict any future KgNB that will be used between the same UE and another gNB after n or more handovers (since NH parameters are only computable by the UE and the AMF). The AS SMC procedure is for RRC and UP security algorithms negotiation and RRC security activation. When AS security context is to be established in the gNB, the AMF sends the complete UE 5G security capabilities to the gNB (i.e., all bits for every capability defined in TS 24.501 [28] and received in NAS signalling).
