. 16.19.2 Timing and Synchronization 16.19.2.1 Scheduling and Timing To accommodate the propagation delay in ATG, the timing relationship is enhanced by one cell-specific offset : - is a configured scheduling offset that needs to be larger or equal to the max RTT. The scheduling offset is used to allow the UE sufficient processing time between a downlink reception and an uplink transmission, see TS 38.213 [38]. 16.19.2.2 Timing Advance For the serving cell, the network broadcasts coarse gNB location information. The UE shall have valid GNSS position as well as gNB location before connecting to an ATG cell. To achieve synchronisation, before and during connection to an ATG cell, the UE shall compute the RTT between UE and the gNB based on the GNSS position and the gNB location parameters, and autonomously pre-compensate the TTA for the RTT between the UE and the gNB.
