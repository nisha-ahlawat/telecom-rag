The L2 U2N Remote UE reports one or multiple candidate L2 U2N Relay UE(s) and Uu measurements, after it measures/discovers the candidate L2 U2N Relay UE(s): - The L2 U2N Remote UE filters the appropriate L2 U2N Relay UE(s) according to relay selection criteria before reporting. SD-RSRP is used as sidelink measurement quantity. 2. The gNB decides to switch the L2 U2N Remote UE to a target L2 U2N Relay UE.
